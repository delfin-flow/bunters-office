# Recover (P5)

The named flow for a loss: machine, account access, the owner, or the record itself. Goal: hold the
business steady, then restore — fast, safe, and never by guessing (`charters/pipelines.md` P5).

1. **Triage what was lost** — one of: the machine, access to a specific account, the owner (death /
   incapacity), or the record itself. The first action differs for each.
2. **Protect what still works.** Nothing destructive in the first 24 hours; secure cash and customer data;
   everything else can wait (`START-HERE.md`). Use the restore order (the bundle's `recovery/restore-order.md`
   and `store/procedures/`), which says what to bring back first.
3. **Recover access lawfully.** Use `recovery_route` and the authority map, never a guessed password and
   never a login as the owner (`charters/authority.md`, `charters/secrets.md`). Where a professional is
   needed, stop and refer.
4. **Rebuild the record if it was lost.** If `store/` is gone, restore it from its own repository remote
   (`settings.yaml → package_remote`) or from a bundle via `skills/import-bundle`.
5. **Re-verify and log.** Mark every entry re-confirmed with a date; log the recovery in `store/log.md`.
   A recovery that is not logged did not happen, for the next person.
