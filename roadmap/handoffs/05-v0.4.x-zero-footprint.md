# 05 — v0.4.x: the host stays as it was (S19)

**Delivered**
- `charters/footprint.md`: no package installs, no dotfiles/`PATH`, no services or scheduled tasks, no files
  outside the repository; scratch and caches only in git-ignored `build/` and `.tmp/`.
- Exceptions are permissioned and recorded in `store/footprint.md` (what, where, why, when, removal, who).
- `uninstall.sh` prints every recorded dependency with its removal command, or states that nothing outside
  the two folders was added; `status.sh` reports the register.

**Decisions & deviations**
- The claim is a **permission gate plus a register**, not a guarantee: nothing diffs `$HOME` before and after
  (S19 says so explicitly rather than implying a check that does not exist).

**Known issues**
- `v0.4.0` shipped a printer that stripped spaces from the removal command (`sudoaptremovex`); fixed in
  `v0.4.1`, and the class of bug — normalising a value with an output transform — is worth remembering.

**Hand-off to next story**
The next real test is a person installing this on a machine that is not the author's, and a second person
following the printed document. Both are S5, and both are unproven.
