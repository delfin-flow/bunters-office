# 07 — v0.5.1: access resilience and infrastructure hardening (S27)

**Delivered**
- `templates/procedures/access-resilience.md` — second admin, parallel 2FA devices, backup codes, quick
  regain path; `provider-successor-contacts.md` — Google IAM, Apple Legacy Contact, Microsoft, Meta; and
  `incident-first-24h.md` — the first-day runbook.
- `templates/inventory/billing-cards.csv` — the register that stops auto-renew from dying silently.
- `status.sh` — flags a billing card expiring within 60 days or expired, and flags chain accounts that
  share one recovery route (single point of failure).
- Bundle carries `billing-cards.csv`; manifest counts it.
- Two more drill tasks (second-admin access, next expiring card) and two cadence windows
  (recovery-channel send/receive test, card-expiry check).

**Decisions & deviations**
- D24 records the reasoning. Everything is Markdown + small `status.sh` additions — no new dependencies,
  consistent with the "scripts, not binaries" rule.
- The single-point-of-failure audit is an exact-duplicate check on `recovery_route` only; it does not
  yet detect routes that *differ* in text but resolve to the same mailbox. That is the honest scope today.

**Known issues**
- `status.sh` reads billing-card expiry as `YYYY-MM`; a card row with no expiry is flagged as a gap rather
  than silently passed.

**Hand-off to next story**
S5 (a real drill) now has three extra tasks: domain lockdown, bundle-import return trip, and second-admin
access. S4 (authority gate) and S6 (reminders without a daemon) remain the closest unproven rungs.
